F-01

Live session tracking

Markers and memory locations become songs and sections automatically. No re-entry, no second source of truth.

F-02

Operator panel

A control surface for the playback engineer. Transport is guarded — nothing fires unless you arm it.

F-03

Running order, in the app

Set changed at soundcheck? Drag. Done. Your session never moves — no marker gets renamed, and the new order survives a resync and a restart.

F-04PRO

LAN browser view

Your band opens a browser. That's the whole setup — every phone and tablet on the network gets the stage display, up to 24 at once, with nothing to install.

F-05PRO

Main / backup redundancy

If your Mac dies, the show doesn't have to. The backup mirrors your running order and chases its own timecode all night — you promote it by hand when you need it.

F-06

Layout editor

Decide what each display shows — bar countdown, section, tempo, key, timecode, band logo — and where it sits.

Playback is sacred.

The operator panel never sends a transport command you didn't ask for. Play is confirmed, stop is armed, and everything you press is logged with timecode.

■ It stops itself at song end.

A SongEnd marker arms an automatic stop — the session halts cleanly on the downbeat, every night, without a human finger on the spacebar.
